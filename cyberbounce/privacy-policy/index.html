Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 2026

At CyberBounce, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.
01

Information We Collect

CyberBounce itself does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location. However, our app uses third-party services that may collect certain data as described below.

02

Advertising — Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the app. AdMob may automatically collect and use the following data from your device:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • IP address and general location
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App usage and interaction data

This data is collected by Google to serve relevant ads. You can learn more at: policies.google.com/privacy

⚙ Opt-Out Instructions To opt out of personalized ads on Android: go to Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
03

Local & Cloud Data Storage

Some gameplay data such as your preferences is saved locally on your device. To power the global leaderboard, your score is stored securely in the cloud. We do not share this data with any third parties.

04

Children's Privacy

CyberBounce is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

05

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
